# Third-party notices

## Photographs and research

Individual photographs retain their own licences. `research/media-manifest.json` records each author, source page, original URL, licence code/URL and modification note. The visitor interface shows attribution. Original photographs and generated display files are excluded from Git and recreated using the media script. Reuse must follow each image's licence, including applicable attribution and share-alike conditions.

Historical claims link to their sources through the research dataset and database citations. A reference to a source is not a grant of rights to reproduce its complete text or images.

## Maps and fonts

The local basemap uses public-domain land, lake, and river geometry from Natural Earth’s 1:50m vector release v5.1.2. The bundled GeoJSON retains features around India and rounds coordinates to four decimal places. Monument markers use approximate locations. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

The interface requests Google Fonts with local/system fallbacks. Fonts and external services retain their respective terms and licences.

## Software

Dependencies and resolved versions are recorded in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`. React, Express, PostgreSQL tooling, Leaflet, markercluster, Sharp and other dependencies retain their respective licences. Installing dependencies does not transfer their ownership or remove their licence obligations.

No broad open-source licence has been added for Vastukala project code in this private first version.
